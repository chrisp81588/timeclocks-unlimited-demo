Shipping & Deliveries

How we get your order out the door — and where to go if you need a hand.

Shipping method

We ship via UPS Ground on orders. Most in-stock items ship within 1–2 business days of your order.

Free shipping

Orders over $99 ship free. For orders under that, or for a shipping quote on a specific item before you buy, just give us a call.

Questions about your order

Because larger systems (paging, break bell controllers) can ship freight rather than parcel, the fastest way to get an exact cost or delivery estimate is a quick call — we'll quote it before you check out, not after.
